# Natural Selection

topics:
  - Biology
  - Science > Biology
  - Science > Evolution

Natural selection is a process through which heritable characteristics that affect reproductive success can become more or less common within a population.

It is one of the principal mechanisms of [[Evolution]].

## Variation

Individuals within a population differ from one another.

Some of these differences are heritable.

## Differential reproduction

If a heritable characteristic affects an organism's ability to survive or reproduce, that characteristic can influence the composition of subsequent generations.

## Relationship to evolution

Natural selection is not synonymous with evolution.

Evolution is a broader concept describing changes in populations over generations. Natural selection is one mechanism capable of producing those changes.
