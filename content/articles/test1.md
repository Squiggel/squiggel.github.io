# Evolution

topics:
  - Biology
  - Science > Biology
  - Philosophy > Philosophy of Science

Evolution is the change in the heritable characteristics of biological populations over successive generations.

The modern understanding of evolution combines ideas from [[Natural Selection]], genetics, ecology and population biology.

## Natural selection

Natural selection is one of the mechanisms that can produce evolutionary change.

Organisms vary in their characteristics, and some of those characteristics affect survival and reproduction.

Charles Darwin described natural selection in [[On the Origin of Species]].

## Evidence

Evidence for evolution comes from several areas of biology, including:

- comparative anatomy
- palaeontology
- genetics
- biogeography
- developmental biology

For an overview, see [the Encyclopaedia Britannica article on evolution](https://www.britannica.com/science/evolution-scientific-theory).
